# DCH 3-part series: storyboard

Channel: DCH LinkedIn company page only. Format: 1080x1920, 3 parts, 8 to 13 seconds each. Source: DCH Company Brochure 2026 (all on-screen text is verbatim from it). Status: plan only, nothing built or published.

## 1. Hook formula and why

Hook family: bold statement. Closest formula: #14 Pattern Interrupt, a short line that works alone on frame 0: "Growth breaks in the gaps."

- Honest: it is the brochure's own page heading, no number, result or client is claimed.
- Trap check (line must not mean nothing without the post): it works alone, and the next two lines in the same part explain it with the brochure's own examples.
- Promise: one. The video will show where growth breaks and what closes the gap.
- Story shape: problem, cause, fix.

How this differs from the existing 5-part series (gap, engine, proof, method, next step): this cut folds gap into one hook, folds engine and its AI payoff into one story, and merges next step with the sign-off. It drops proof, method and the five founder types entirely. The hook is shown through a visual (five disconnected nodes), and the hero line "Growth made possible." is the sign-off. Note: the old series files are not in the repo, so this was checked by the brief only (see unconfirmed).

Hero line: "Growth made possible." No retired line is used anywhere.

## 2. Series system

- Canvas 1080x1920. Safe area: y 300 to 1500, x 120 to 960 (right 120 px and bottom 320 px clear of platform UI).
- Ground #0F131A, card #161B24, text #EFF4F4, muted #8D97A8, teal #4ED0CC primary. Orange #E0793E used once, on the final CTA item in Part 3 only.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 headlines, Poppins body, JetBrains Mono for the counter and labels. All fonts and the logo are local files. Square corners, blueprint grid, soft teal glow.
- Counter top-left in mono: "01 / 03", "02 / 03", "03 / 03", with a short label (THE GAP, ONE ENGINE, NEXT STEP).
- Teal thread: a 6 px vertical line at x = 64 (left margin, clear of the text box at x 120). It runs the full height in every part. A brighter pulse travels down it to hand off.
- Reading time floor: 0.5 s + 0.3 s per word. Fast in, then hold. Last line held at least 2 s. No em-dashes anywhere.
- Music: one bed across all parts, each part's data-media-start = sum of previous parts. Sparse SFX on reveals.

# Brag Plan: Digital Creatives Hub (DCH)

## What is this app?
The marketing site of DCH, a business development creative agency: brand, platforms and people, with AI in every layer, entered through a free 4-minute Growth Diagnostic.

## The angle
The site's own positioning line is the whole video: "Projects end. Momentum doesn't." Most agencies hand over a deliverable and leave; DCH builds growth that keeps moving. The video enacts that: the first line stops dead, the second one keeps going.

## Hook (0-4.5s)
"Projects end." sits alone and still on the dark ruled plane. A beat later: "Momentum doesn't." arrives, "Momentum" in teal, and a thin line starts travelling.

## Key moments
- The Growth Diagnostic (the site's front door): "Take the free diagnostic" button gets pressed; the intake card shows "A startup founder" selected, then "Brand: strategy, identity, story" selected.
- "Free · 4 minutes · see where you stand before you talk to anyone."
- The four stages draw along a timeline one by one: Diagnose. Architect. Build. Compound. (last one in orange, as on the site), each with its real line.

## Outro / punchline
"Strategy. Creativity. Growth. Without limits." sign-off, DCH logo, "Take the free Growth Diagnostic".

## User flow worth showing
Entry: hero "Growth made possible." with the diagnostic CTA. Key action: choose who you are and what you need in the Diagnostic. Result: the path appears as four stages, ending in Compound.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, soft crossfades and slides, confidence through restraint; no jokes.

## Format: vertical, 1080x1920
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0F131A (hsl 220 25% 8%), grid "blueprint" lines + grain
- Accent: teal #4ED0CC (primary), orange #E0793E (secondary, used once, on "Compound.")
- Text: #EFF4F4, muted #8D97A8
- Display font: Bricolage Grotesque; body Poppins; labels JetBrains Mono
- Strongest visual element: square-cornered cards on the ruled blueprint plane, the four-stage timeline

## Share copy (draft)
Projects end. Momentum doesn't. Here's how we build brand, platforms and people that keep moving, with AI in every layer. Start with the free Growth Diagnostic.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9 (CC BY 4.0, Sascha Ende, ende.app)
- Music treatment: in at 0s low, steady, fade out over final 2s
- Music cue guidance: vol-9 ~114.8 BPM; strong cues 4.23s, 6.34s, 10.54s, 12.65s, 23.17s (use 4.2 for "Momentum", 12.6 for stage reveal start)
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass/RMS breathes the background glow. No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse; soft UI click on selections, soft tick per stage
- Restraint rule: nothing louder than the music; no stingers.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 4.5s
Black-blue plane, grid fades in. "Projects end." (hold ~1.6s settled). Then "Momentum doesn't." with "Momentum" teal; a hairline draws across the screen.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two lines one after another.
Audio intent: held breath, then the bed opens.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The front door — 6s
Hero lockup "Growth made possible." (possible. teal), CTA button "Take the free diagnostic" gets a simulated press. Diagnostic card slides up: "A startup founder" selected, then "Brand: strategy, identity, story" selected. Caption: "Free · 4 minutes".
Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor tap on CTA, two option selects ~1.2s apart.
Audio intent: gentle clicks.
Transition mood: slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The path — 6s
Headline "Diagnose. Architect. Build. Compound." Four stages draw one by one along a line with their real lines (hold each ~0.9s settled; final set holds ~2s). Compound in orange.
Sequential/interaction: yes — four items, snapped to every other beat.
Audio intent: steady forward motion.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Sign-off — 3.5s
Logo, "Strategy. Creativity. Growth. Without limits.", button "Take the free Growth Diagnostic". Music fades.
Audio intent: resolve.

**Music mood:** upbeat, restrained
**Audio summary:** quiet open, steady business bed, soft clicks, fade at sign-off.
